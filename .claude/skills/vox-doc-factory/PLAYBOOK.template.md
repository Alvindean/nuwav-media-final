# PLAYBOOK — [CHANNEL NAME]

Copy this to the channel root as `PLAYBOOK.md`. Read at the start of **every** run,
before Stage 1. Every active rule is binding.

**Cap: 25 active rules.** At the cap, adding one retires the weakest-evidence rule.
That constraint is the point — a playbook nobody can hold in their head is wallpaper.

**Format.** A rule needs evidence. An opinion is not a rule.

```md
## R00N — [one-line imperative]
Evidence: [episode, metric, number, comparison]
Applies: [stage, and where in it]
Status: Proposed | Confirmed | Contested
Added: EPnn.  Confirmed: EPnn, EPnn.
```

**Status ladder:** Proposed (seen once) → Confirmed (held 3+ episodes) → Contested (a later
episode disagreed; test deliberately) → Retired (failed twice after confirmation).

Keep retired rules with their reasons, or the same bad idea gets rediscovered every quarter.
